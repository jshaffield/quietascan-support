Quieta Scan access

Help for turning paper schedules into calendar events.

Quieta Scan is separate from the full Quieta family app. It uses Quieta Pro only to verify access and does not use Quieta family sync, Stream, Q codes, or Quieta event storage.

Core flow Unlock Quieta Scan, scan a flyer, review extracted event drafts, then save approved events to Apple Calendar.
Simulator review Use Sample Flyer on simulator. VisionKit document scanning is available on physical iPhone devices.
Permissions Camera is used for document scanning. Calendar permission is requested only when you export events.
Data Flyer text is processed for calendar drafting on the device. Quieta Scan access is checked through RevenueCat.

How do I unlock Quieta Scan?

Subscribe to Quieta Scan, restore purchases, or use your Quieta Pro identity to unlock scanning and calendar export.

How do I test scanning?

On a physical iPhone, tap Start Scan and point the camera at a flyer or printed schedule. On simulator, tap Sample Flyer to load a representative schedule into the review queue.

Why does calendar export ask for permission?

Quieta Scan uses Apple EventKit to save reviewed events. iOS requires calendar permission before any event can be written.

Is this the same product as Quieta?

No. Quieta Scan is a focused utility for flyer-to-calendar capture. It does not include Quieta family sync, shared Stream, Q codes, or Quieta-owned event storage.

Where can I ask for help?

Email support@quieta.co with “Quieta Scan” in the subject line and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
